# Design System: English Center Manager System
**Project ID:** 17313629417379770276

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Modern, Professional, Clean, with generous whitespace and a sophisticated aesthetic. The interface should feel structured and organized, utilizing subtle shadows and a restricted, premium palette.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
* **Primary Action:** Deep Ocean Blue (#1d4ed8) - Used for primary buttons and main navigation highlights.
* **Secondary Action:** Slate Gray (#64748b) - Used for secondary buttons and borders.
* **Success/Accent:** Emerald Green (#10b981) - Used for success messages or positive trends.
* **Background:** Off-white Slate (#f8fafc) - Main app background. 
* **Surface/Cards:** Pure White (#ffffff) - Foreground card surfaces to stand out against the slate background.

## 3. Typography Rules
* Font families: Modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Roboto).
* Headers should be crisp and high-contrast, body text should be easily readable (slate gray).

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Gently rounded corners (`rounded-lg`), Deep blue background with white text, micro-interactions on hover.
* **Containers/Cards:** White background, gently rounded corners (`rounded-lg`), whisper-soft, diffused shadows (`shadow-sm` or `shadow-md`) for gentle elevation.
* **Inputs:** Clean form fields with subtle light-gray borders, highlighting deep blue on focus.

## 5. Layout Principles
* Clean grid systems and flexbox layouts.
* Generous padding and margins between sections to ensure a focused, distraction-free experience.
